# The World Today

The God-King rules the kingdom of Elandros and the imperial lands of Aurelion through divine immortality, Dragonborn nobility, institutional terror, and control over the stories people are allowed to know.

## Political Structure
Summary: Aurelion is governed through immortal rule, Dragonborn noble houses, legal enforcers, and identity-stripped servants of the crown.

### God-King
Role: Eternal ruler of Aurelion
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/elandros_sigil_icon_only.png

The God-King was once an elf. During the Shattering he betrayed his people, stole Eternity, and bound it in a gem to become immortal.

His rule has lasted for more than two thousand years. He claims divine legitimacy, uses Dragonborn nobility as his ruling class, and hides or rewrites the truth of the Seraph.

### Obligators
Role: Judges, juries, legal overseers, and noble auditors
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/obligator-white-robes.png

Obligators are the God-King's law. They judge disputes, enforce imperial doctrine, oversee city rulers, and keep the noble families inside the throne's authority.

They are Dragonborn who have exhibited extreme loyalty.

### Inquisitors
Role: Extreme enforcers of imperial punishment
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/grand inquisitor.png

Inquisitors are elves bred and experimented on by the God-King. Nullite spikes are driven through their ears, their eyes are removed, and their mouths are burned shut.

The Nullite strips away identity and lets the God-King impose his will. A golden mask is melted and fused onto each face to hide the elven features beneath.

### Elven Thralls
Role: Failed Inquisitor experiments and slave labor in the Pit
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/elven_thrall.png

Elven thralls are failed experiments discarded into the Pit. Nullite spikes were driven through their eyes, their mouths were burned shut, and they were left to serve Dominion and the Warden.

They are both victims of the God-King's breeding program and tools of the empire's deepest cruelty.

### Marshals
Role: Imperial police and city enforcers
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/marshal_full.png

Marshals are loyal Dragonborn and, rarely, other citizens who prove absolute devotion to the throne.

They enforce the God-King's laws, carry out Obligators' rulings, and keep the lower classes under watch.

## Drakensport
Summary: The capital is a colossal tiered citadel carved into the Skyfang Mountains.

### First Ring
Role: Outermost labor ring
Image:

Farmers, factory workers, and laborers live here in misery. Their toil feeds the upper rings while surveillance and hunger keep them compliant.

### Second Ring
Role: Craftsmen and artisans
Image:

This ring is better off than the outer farms and factories, but it is still heavily taxed and constantly watched by Inquisitors.

### Third and Fourth Rings
Role: Lesser noble domains
Image:

The six lesser noble families keep their power bases here. Markets, offices, bureaucratic courts, and noble keeps make these rings a nest of political intrigue.

### Fifth and Sixth Rings
Role: High noble estates and military preparation
Image:

Luxury estates, Inquisitor training barracks, and elite drilling grounds occupy these tiers. They are close enough to the Citadel to feel the God-King's favor and fear.

### Seventh Ring
Role: The Citadel
Image:

The seventh ring holds the God-King's fortress-palace. Gold and obsidian towers rise above the smog as symbols of divine tyranny.

## The Six Great Cities
Summary: Each of the six most powerful Dragonborn families rules a major city that feeds the empire's wealth, weapons, faith, secrets, and violence.

### Ebonmere
Ruler: House Veynar, Black Dragonborn
Specialty: Trade, obsidian quarries, smuggling, black markets
Image:

Ebonmere is the River of Obsidian: wealthy, corrupt, and crawling with spies. House Veynar rules through trade leverage, criminal markets, and blackmail.

### Silverhold
Ruler: House Drakov, Red Dragonborn
Specialty: Silver and mithril mining, weaponsmithing
Image:

Silverhold is the Mountain Forge. It is harsh, unforgiving, and built on dangerous labor that treats people as expendable as ore.

### Thalmyre
Ruler: House Ardyn, Blue Dragonborn
Specialty: Magic, scholarship, arcane libraries
Image:

Thalmyre is the Seat of Knowledge. Its scholars and mages are brilliant, censored, and watched because knowledge is power and power is dangerous.

### Ravenshade
Ruler: House Caelthorne, Green Dragonborn
Specialty: Espionage, assassins, thieves' guilds
Image:

Ravenshade is the Shrouded City. It is shadowy, manipulative, and full of political scheming. Few secrets escape its web.

### Brightmarch
Ruler: House Solmyre, Gold Dragonborn
Specialty: Navy, radiant temples, religious propaganda
Image:

Brightmarch is the Sunlit Coast. Its rulers preach divine favor, command the navy, and use radiant doctrine to control the masses.

### Ironwell
Ruler: House Gravenforge, Bronze Dragonborn
Specialty: Iron foundries, mines, prison labor, military industry
Image:

Ironwell is the City of Chains. It is brutal, smoky, and devoted to the weapons and labor systems that keep Elandros strong.

## The Ruins of Irillis
Summary: Irillis is preserved as a wound and a warning, though the truth of its fall is older and stranger than imperial propaganda admits.

### Irillis
Role: Destroyed elven city and preserved warning
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/Irillis_Signet.png

Irillis was once a city of trade and culture, ruled by the Cindralis family of elves. It stood against the God-King and was erased in fire and steel.

The ruins remain untouched by decree. Survivors and descendants were chained and sent to Ironwell and the southern work camps, where they labor within sight of their broken city.

Imperial propaganda dates the Irilli uprising to nearly one thousand years ago. The deeper lore places the city's destruction in the aftermath of the Shattering more than two thousand years ago, making the public timeline suspect.

## Imperial Propaganda
Summary: Official histories praise the God-King as eternal, frame the lower races as naturally subordinate, and turn Irillis into a lesson against rebellion.

### The Nytheris Dissemination
Role: Daily imperial publication
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/elandros_sigil_icon_only.png

The Nytheris Dissemination teaches that the God-King's rule is without beginning or end, that Dragonborn nobility preserves order, and that lesser races labor in their rightful place.

Its version of history says Irillis rebelled nearly one thousand years ago and was rightly destroyed. It presents the ruins and the suffering of the Irilli people as proof of the God-King's mercy and wrath.

The publication's closing doctrine is simple: so it has been for two thousand years, and so it shall be forever.
