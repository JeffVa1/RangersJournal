# Timeline

## The Seraph and the Elves
Era: More than 2000 years ago - Before the Shattering
Summary: The Seraph was the god worshiped by the elves, and its power stood at the center of their oldest faith.
Links:

The elves eventually chose to contain the Seraph. The reason has been lost, hidden, or deliberately erased, but the decision changed the shape of the world. They did not set out to simply kill their god; they believed they could bind and preserve it.

To make the attempt possible, the elves forged seven divine weapons. Each weapon was formidable alone, but together they held the power to shatter or kill a god.

## The Seven Weapons Are Raised
Era: More than 2000 years ago - The Shattering
Summary: The elves used the seven god-killing weapons in a single ritual strike against the Seraph.
Links:

The ritual shattered the Seraph instead of containing it whole. Its divine substance, soul, and true name broke apart across the world.

The weapons were spent by the act. Their god-killing power would need time to recharge before they could threaten an immortal being again.

## Identity Is Trapped
Era: More than 2000 years ago - The Shattering
Summary: The elves tried to hold the entire Seraph inside their largest gemstone, but only Identity was successfully contained.
Links:

The gemstone was enormous, nearly the size of a house, and the elves believed it could act as a vessel for divine power. They were wrong about the scale of what they could command.

Identity became trapped inside the gem. Since then, it has remained largely dormant, endlessly pondering itself, the world, the elves, its creator, and the ruined whole it came from.

## The True Name Breaks
Era: More than 2000 years ago - The Shattering
Summary: The Seraph's true name split into seven syllables, each becoming a divine aspect with its own will.
Links:

Eternity, Dominion, Radiance, Memory, Awakening, Identity, and Whimsy each sought a home or purpose after the Shattering.

The Seraph's body became Nullite, a rare metal that devours identity and suppresses magic. Its unbound soul became blue crystal, a raw identity-bearing power scattered through the land.

## The God-King Betrays the Elves
Era: More than 2000 years ago - The First Betrayal
Summary: An elf who would become the God-King stole Eternity during the Shattering and used it to become immortal.
Links:

He bound Eternity within a great gem and drew on its power. Through it, he gained immortality and a portion of the Seraph's authority over endlessness.

The elves recognized the betrayal too late. Their divine weapons had not recovered from shattering the Seraph, and without them they could not overcome the new immortal.

## The Aspects Take Root
Era: More than 2000 years ago - The First Scattering
Summary: The remaining aspects moved into the deepest and strangest homes the world could offer.
Links:

Dominion attempted to conquer the planet by driving itself into the world's core. It fell short, and the place above it would eventually become the Pit.

Radiance sought the most radiant home and claimed the rising sun. Memory became one with the flow of time itself. Awakening entered the Great Wyrm, the ancient progenitor of dragons. Whimsy diffused itself through living creatures and also took root in Biigs, a broken man it mistook for a simple creature.

## Irillis Falls
Era: More than 2000 years ago - The God-King's Retribution
Summary: The God-King destroyed Irillis after the elves failed to kill him, and the city became the warning at the heart of his reign.
Links: [Part I recaps](/session-recaps/part-1)

The God-King allied with Awakening through the Great Wyrm and crushed the elves. Most of the elven people were killed, enslaved, or folded into his later breeding programs.

In the midst of the catastrophe, the elves created the Scrolls of Irillis and a map to the hidden weapons. They used Identity, still trapped in the gem, to seal both the prophecy and the map for a future generation.

## Dragonborn Rule Begins
Era: The First Imperial Age - The Eternal Reign
Summary: After rebellion and elven resistance, the God-King raised loyal Dragonborn bloodlines into power across Elandros.
Links:

Dragonborn nobles became the ruling class of the empire. The Great Six took control of the major cities, while the Lesser Six governed the capital's machinery of food, coin, law, guard command, propaganda, and noble ambition.

The God-King's divine bond with Eternity and the Great Wyrm's bond with Awakening gave the throne unnatural authority over dragons and Dragonborn.

## The Witches Discover Whimsy
Era: A few hundred years ago - Hidden Witchcraft
Summary: A coven of hags discovered Whimsy within living creatures and learned to extract it.
Links:

The witches used their magic to pull Whimsy out of creatures and bind it into themselves. Their craft lets them channel Whimsy through Nullite and blue crystal in ways most scholars would not understand.

They are strongly associated with wild magic and with practices that bend identity, chaos, and living will.

## The Pit Feeds the Empire
Era: The current era
Summary: Dominion's resting place has become the Pit, where Nullite is extracted under the Warden's rule.
Links: [Part II recaps](/session-recaps/part-2)

The Pit is more than a mine. It is a wound above Dominion, filled with failed Inquisitor experiments, elven thralls, prison labor, and the machinery of imperial cruelty.

Nullite deposits form where a strong concentration of an aspect tries to create a body, or where the body of the Seraph reacts like an anti-matter pressure against divine power.

## Blue Crystal Becomes a Bridge
Era: The current era
Summary: Professor Elric Vaunt has learned to use refined blue crystal as a bridge between locations.
Links:

Blue crystal carries raw identity and soul-force from the Seraph. It is not tied to a single aspect, does not form around Nullite deposits, and remains extremely difficult to find.

Vaunt's breakthrough proves the crystal can bridge locations through teleportation, though its deeper properties are still poorly understood.
