# Timeline

This timeline uses broad eras rather than exact calendar dates. The oldest events are intentionally approximate because imperial histories, elven records, and living memory disagree.

## The Shattering and the First Betrayal
Era: 2000 years ago
Summary: The elves shattered the Seraph, its true name broke into seven divine syllables, Identity was trapped, Eternity was stolen, and the hope of Irillis was sealed away for the future.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/seraph-shattering-ritual.png :: The ritual that broke the Seraph :: The seven divine weapons raised against the elves' god. | /assets/WorldLoreArt/scrolls-of-irillis-web.png :: The Scrolls of Irillis :: The sealed warning and map left for those who would finish Irillis's work.
Links:

The elves decided to contain the Seraph for reasons that have been lost, hidden, or deliberately erased. They forged seven god-killing weapons for the attempt. Each weapon was formidable alone, but together they carried the force needed to shatter or kill a god.

The ritual did not contain the Seraph whole. Its body became Nullite. Its unbound soul scattered as blue crystal. Its true name split into seven syllables: Eternity, Dominion, Radiance, Memory, Awakening, Identity, and Whimsy.

Identity was the only aspect the elves successfully trapped in their house-sized gemstone. At the same time, the elf who would become the God-King betrayed his people and stole Eternity, binding it in a great gem to claim immortality.

The remaining aspects took root across the world. Dominion drove itself toward the planet's core and fell short. Radiance claimed the rising sun. Memory joined the flow of time. Awakening entered the Great Wyrm. Whimsy diffused through living creatures and took unusual root in Biigs.

The seven divine weapons were spent by the Shattering and needed long years to recover. The elves hid them and created a map to their resting place. They also created the Scrolls of Irillis, using Identity in the gem to seal both prophecy and map until future champions could act.

## Irillis Falls and the Eternal Reign Begins
Era: 2000 years ago
Summary: The God-King survived the elves' retaliation, allied with the Great Wyrm, destroyed Irillis, and raised the first shape of his eternal empire.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/Irillis_Signet.png :: The signet of Irillis :: A surviving emblem of the elven city the God-King turned into a warning.
Links:

When the elves realized Eternity had been stolen, they tried to kill the traitor who had become immortal. Their divine weapons had not recovered from the Shattering, and without them the God-King could not be slain.

In retribution, he allied with Awakening through the Great Wyrm and destroyed Irillis. Most elves were killed, enslaved, or pushed into systems that would later become the God-King's breeding programs and experiments.

The God-King's rule began from the ruins. His bond with Eternity made him deathless, while the Great Wyrm's bond with Awakening gave the throne unnatural influence over dragons and Dragonborn. Dragonborn bloodlines would become the empire's ruling class.

## The Witches Steal Whimsy
Era: ~450 years ago
Summary: A coven of hags discovered Whimsy in living creatures and learned to extract it into themselves.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/Biigs.png :: Biigs :: The strange immortal who carries a growing piece of Whimsy.
Links:

Whimsy had already spread through the creatures of the land, granting the world a measure of chaos through living will. Biigs carried a larger and stranger piece because Whimsy mistook his broken mind for that of a simple creature.

Roughly four and a half centuries ago, a coven of hags discovered Whimsy's presence in living creatures. They learned to pull it from those creatures and bind it into themselves.

Their craft lets them channel Whimsy through Nullite and blue crystal in ways most scholars would not understand. They are strongly tied to wild magic and to practices that bend identity, chaos, and will.

## Dominion and the Birth of the Pit
Era: ~300 years ago
Summary: The God-King found Dominion's resting place and began turning the wound above it into the Pit, the empire's deepest prison and Nullite mine.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/the-pit-nullite-mine.png :: The Pit :: The mine, prison, and divine wound dug above Dominion.
Links:

Dominion had tried to conquer the world by driving itself into the planet's core, but it fell short. For centuries its resting place remained hidden beneath stone, pressure, and divine influence.

Roughly three centuries ago, the God-King found it. The excavation above Dominion became the Pit: a mine, prison, forge, and punishment engine built around Nullite extraction.

The Pit is where failed Inquisitor experiments are discarded, where elven thralls are worked until nothing remains, and where the Warden's rule intersects with Dominion's pressure below.

## Vaunt Refines the Blue Crystal
Era: ~15 years ago
Summary: Professor Elric Vaunt discovered the properties of blue crystal and spent years refining it into a bridge between locations.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/blue-crystal-cluster.png :: Blue crystal :: Raw Seraph soul-force refined into impossible travel.
Links:

Blue crystal contains raw identity and soul-force from the Seraph. It is not tied to a single aspect, does not form around Nullite deposits, and remains extremely difficult to find.

Professor Elric Vaunt discovered the crystal's deeper properties roughly fifteen years ago. Over years of dangerous study, he refined it into a teleportation bridge between locations.

His work remains poorly understood, but it proves that the Seraph's scattered soul can connect places by identity rather than distance.

## The Fifth Flame Claims the Lost Scrolls
Era: Current Era - Sessions 1-3
Summary: The party fled Drakensport, opened the Crypt of Irillis, passed the trials, and recovered the Lost Scrolls as the God-King's gaze turned toward them.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/scrolls-of-irillis-web.png :: The Lost Scrolls :: The sealed Irilli warning recovered from the crypt.
Links: [Part I recaps](/session-recaps/part-1)

Hammond's call to rebellion sent the party out of Drakensport and toward the ancient crypts. The first road exposed them to Marshals, Inquisitors, old Irilli wards, and the danger of carrying a truth the empire had buried.

Inside the Crypt of Irillis, the party passed trials of sight, speech, and blood. The sealed warning opened, naming them heirs of ruin and warning that the God-King would sense what they had done.

This is the first current-era moment when the ancient work of Irillis moved from prophecy into living hands.

## Brightmarch, the Inquisitor, and Vaunt's Bridge
Era: Current Era - Sessions 4-6
Summary: The party survived Brightmarch's sewers, lost and reclaimed the scrolls, killed an Inquisitor, and escaped through Professor Vaunt's blue-crystal teleportation.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/grand inquisitor.png :: Grand Inquisitor :: The shape of imperial punishment hunting the scrolls. | /assets/WorldLoreArt/blue-crystal-cluster.png :: Blue crystal bridge :: The refined crystal power behind Vaunt's escape circle.
Links: [Part I recaps](/session-recaps/part-1)

Brightmarch became the first major city where the party's actions collided openly with imperial control. A false Hammond, a hidden Dragonborn mage, and an Inquisitor nearly took the Scrolls of Irillis from them.

The party killed the Inquisitor and escaped the Golden Gallon as alarms rang through the city. In the hidden cellar beneath the tavern, Professor Elric Vaunt opened a teleportation circle powered by refined blue crystal.

The escape proved that Vaunt's discovery was not theory. The crystal could move people between places, and the party had now seen one of the Seraph's physical remnants used in living magic.

## The Pit Is Broken
Era: Current Era - Sessions 7-11
Summary: The party descended into the Pit, encountered Dominion, rescued Hammond, freed hundreds of prisoners, and founded a hidden refuge.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/the-pit-nullite-mine.png :: The Pit :: The prison-mine above Dominion. | /assets/WorldLoreArt/elven_thrall.png :: Elven thrall :: One of the identity-stripped victims discarded into the depths.
Links: [Part II recaps](/session-recaps/part-2)

The Raven directed the party toward Ironwell and the Pit. There they found a prison built around Nullite, slave labor, elven thralls, and the buried pressure of Dominion.

Evander encountered a Dominion Echo and was marked by its will. The party rescued Hammond, fought the Warden, survived the crystal dragon born from the forge, and accepted a terrible bargain to free those trapped below.

Hundreds escaped the Pit. The elven thralls were released from Nullite and died free. The survivors later found a defensible cavern, turning the first act of rescue into the beginning of a real resistance.

## A Resistance Takes Root in Ebonmere's Shadow
Era: Current Era - Sessions 12-14
Summary: The party secured the refugee cave, entered Ebonmere, recovered Chronozar secrets, and followed a missing-girl trail into the hag's game.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/Biigs.png :: Whimsy's thread :: Biigs and Whimsy remain tied to the stranger forces moving around the party.
Links:

The freed prisoners needed food, shelter, and allies. The party secured the cave refuge, dealt with Morvath, gathered supplies in Ebonmere, and began turning survival into organization.

Kaelthar and Rellim uncovered old Chronozar clues that pointed toward Thalmyre and the possible survival or return of Kronos Chronozar's work. At the same time, the Godswood trail led the party toward trolls, Nullite, and a hag connected to Whimsy's stolen power.

These events widened the campaign from flight and rescue into a network of political, arcane, and fey consequences.

## Ebonmere Burns and Sylvara's Game Tightens
Era: Current Era - Sessions 15-22
Summary: The Ebonmere arc escalated through noble intrigue, Sylvara's assassins, Seralyth Veynar's assassination, Ace's false execution, and the search for who paid for the fire.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/inquisitor_full.png :: Imperial hunter :: Ebonmere's shadows are still watched by the God-King's servants.
Links:

In Ebonmere, the party became entangled with House Veynar, Sylvara, the Ivory Reserve, Aethon Ardyn, and the Victory Pits. The assassination attempt against Seralyth Veynar pushed the city toward internal crisis.

Ace was framed, captured, and sentenced to execution in the arena. A bronze dragon and the Grand Inquisitor turned the execution into imperial spectacle, but Ace survived by swapping places with a duplicate.

The party reunited, exposed pieces of Sylvara's involvement, and learned that naming the true patron behind the assassination would require walking deeper into her game.

## Treason at the Veynar War Table
Era: Current Era - Session 24
Summary: Rellim and Ace found evidence tying Lady Valera to the conspiracy, and House Veynar's private war erupted inside the estate.
Images: /assets/WorldLoreArt/obligator-white-robes.png :: Imperial law in noble halls :: The machinery of authority waits behind every noble dispute.
Links:

Rellim and Ace infiltrated Lady Valera's chambers and found the green book: hidden letters from Sylvara that tied Valera to the conspiracy against Seralyth and House Veynar.

Nyacir carried the accusation into the war room. Valera answered with contempt, declaring Maelrix weak and turning loyal guards against their own lord.

At the same moment, Kaelthar and Aethon arrived by magic. The evidence had been found, treason had been named aloud, and Ebonmere's ruling house became a battlefield.
