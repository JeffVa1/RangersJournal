# The Seraph

The Seraph was the divine being worshiped by the elves before the empire. When the elves tried to contain it, they shattered its body, soul, and true name into separate powers that still shape the world.

## Nullite
Kind: Body of the Seraph
Syllable: Body
Branch: Physical Elements
Color: #8f96a3
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/nullite-damascus-metal.png
Summary: A rare metal that devours identity, suppresses magic, and lets stronger wills overwrite what remains.

Nullite is the body of the Seraph. It carries anti-magic and anti-identity properties, and it is especially dangerous when used against a person whose sense of self has already been broken.

It forms where there is a strong concentration of a Seraph aspect. The metal appears to be either the Seraph trying to form a body or the Seraph's body reacting against aspect power like a divine anti-matter pressure.

Nullite is the material used in Inquisitor spikes and elven thrall mutilations.

## Blue Crystals
Kind: Soul of the Seraph
Syllable: Soul
Branch: Physical Elements
Color: #56a8ff
Image: /assets/WorldLoreArt/blue-crystal-cluster.png
Summary: Rare crystals containing raw identity and soul-force from the Seraph, usable for refined magic.

Blue crystal is not tied to any single aspect. It is pure identity-bearing power from the Seraph's scattered soul.

The crystals were dispersed throughout the land when the Seraph was shattered. They do not form around Nullite deposits, are hard to locate, and remain poorly understood.

Professor Elric Vaunt has discovered how to refine blue crystal into a bridge between locations, allowing teleportation.

## Eternity
Kind: Aspect
Syllable: ET
Branch: Syllables
Color: #ffffff
Image:
Summary: The aspect stolen by the elf who became the God-King, bound in a great gem to grant immortality.

Eternity is the aspect of endlessness, continuance, and immortal authority. During the Shattering, the future God-King betrayed the elves and seized it for himself.

He trapped Eternity in a large gem and has drawn on its power for more than two thousand years. This gives him immortality and a portion of the Seraph's divine force, though not the full scope of the Seraph itself.

## Dominion
Kind: Aspect
Syllable: DO
Branch: Syllables
Color: #b468ff
Image:
Summary: The aspect that attempted to conquer the world from within and now lies beneath the Pit.

Dominion was born with a conquering instinct. It tried to drive itself into the core of the planet and rule from the center of the world, but it fell short.

The Pit was eventually dug above Dominion's resting place. Nullite is extracted there under the Warden's authority, and the place acts like a brutal intersection of mining, prison labor, and divine pressure.

## Radiance
Kind: Aspect
Syllable: RA
Branch: Syllables
Color: #ffd36a
Image:
Summary: The aspect that sought the most radiant home and became bound to the rising sun.

Radiance chose the dawn. Its power rests in the rising sun and moves through light, brilliance, and divine fire.

Elyndor's patron is Radiance, though he does not yet understand the true nature of that connection.

## Memory
Kind: Aspect
Syllable: MEM
Branch: Syllables
Color: #87c7ff
Image:
Summary: The aspect that became one with the flow of time and now acts as the world's memory.

Memory made time itself its home. Rather than taking a body, it joined the current of history, sequence, recollection, and consequence.

It acts as the memory of the entire world. Its influence is less visible than Dominion or Eternity, but it is woven through every age.

## Awakening
Kind: Aspect
Syllable: AW
Branch: Syllables
Color: #df4d3f
Image:
Summary: The aspect that entered the Great Wyrm, the ancient progenitor of dragons.

Awakening found the most powerful sleeping creature on the planet: the Great Wyrm, ancient progenitor of dragons.

It inhabited the Great Wyrm and became one with it. The God-King later allied with this power, and through that bond the throne gained unnatural influence over dragons and Dragonborn.

## Identity
Kind: Aspect
Syllable: ID
Branch: Syllables
Color: #28d7c8
Image:
Summary: The aspect trapped in the elves' house-sized gemstone during the failed attempt to contain the Seraph.

Identity is the only aspect the elves successfully trapped in their great gemstone. It has not acted directly since the Shattering.

Instead, it has watched and contemplated. It tries to understand itself, the world, the Seraph, the elves, and the meaning of its own existence from inside the gem.

The elves used Identity's prison-gem to seal the Scrolls of Irillis and the map to the seven weapons.

## Whimsy
Kind: Aspect
Syllable: SY
Branch: Syllables
Color: #ff9f2f
Image:
Summary: The aspect that entered living creatures, gave chaos to the world, and took unusual root in Biigs.

Whimsy chose the creatures of the land. Every creature, great or small, carries some piece of it, and that presence gives the world a measure of chaos through free will.

At the moment Whimsy spread, one man was so beaten down and broken in mind that Whimsy mistook him for a simple creature and inhabited him as well. He came to be known as Biigs.

Through Biigs's consciousness, Whimsy has grown for more than two thousand years. Biigs is effectively immortal, though he does not hold the same concentrated divine force the God-King draws from Eternity.

A few hundred years ago, a coven of hags discovered Whimsy in living creatures and learned to extract it. They imbued it into themselves and now channel Whimsy through Nullite and blue crystal, often through wild magic.
