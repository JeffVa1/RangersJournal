# The Pit

## Session 7 - 11/12/25

### The Arrival of the Raven

Three days passed in the safehouse beneath the Sleeping Dragon Inn. The party waited, uncertain of the resistance leader they had been told to meet—expecting a scarred veteran, some hardened commander forged by war.

What arrived was not what they anticipated.

When the resistance’s commander finally appeared, Ace rushed outside in excitement. The moment he realized their “boss” was a woman, his enthusiasm soured into crude arrogance. The Raven did not acknowledge him once. She passed by him as if he were a fly buzzing at her ear.

Inside, she asked for the scrolls. Caelir immediately pointed to Ranger. Ace shouted the same a moment later, louder, desperate for attention. She ignored him again.

The group moved to the stables for privacy. Ranger studied her closely for any hint of Inquisitorial deception—posture, spells, insignia, enchantments. Nothing. Ace confirmed no malicious magic clung to her. Only then did Ranger withdraw the scrolls from within his tunic, keeping a protective hand over them as he set them down.

With a simple wave of her hand, the scrolls vanished.

Her expression darkened.“There may be more to these than any of you realize.”

### A Choice of Paths

The scrolls’ disappearance sparked heated debate. Should they pursue Hammond—whose last known location was uncertain, possibly Drakensport… or the Pit, Ironwell’s infamous underground prison? Or should they race toward Brightmarch to rescue Professor Vaunt and whatever cure he had discovered?

The Raven ended the argument abruptly. A prisoner transport was departing Drakensport—bound for Ironwell. If they wished to help the professor and intercept the flow of prisoners, this was their moment.

### The Ambush on the Mountain Road

They prepared an ambush along a narrow mountain road carved into the hillside. Evander dislodged a massive boulder, sending it crashing into the wagons’ path. Kaelthar followed by freezing the wheel of the lead wagon solid, locking it in place.

Ranger struck first, firing an arrow through a soldier’s chest before he could cry out. Elyndor slipped behind another and drove his blade upward with surgical accuracy.

Then Evander shouted into the chaos:

“Surrender, or you’ll all get it up the ass!”

Shockingly, the remaining guards threw down their weapons immediately.

Evander bound one guard while Ranger calmed and led the horses away. Ace scavenged their weapons and armor. One prisoner bolted—Ace tore after him swinging wildly with his poleaxe, but missed. Ranger’s magic also failed to stop the man.

The Raven simply raised her hand, clenched her fist, and manifested a shimmering golden spectral hand that wrapped around the fleeing guard, pinning him as though he weighed nothing.

### The Dragon’s Shadow

Then everything changed.

A massive shadow swept over the road, swallowing it in darkness. The air went still. The sunlight dimmed.

A colossal dragon soared overhead—its wings blotting out the sky like a living stormcloud.

Ranger’s warning cut through the terror:

“Dragon! We move NOW!”

They shoved the surrendered guards into their own wagon cage and forced the horses into a frantic gallop, fleeing the exposed road before the dragon’s gaze returned.

### The Professor’s Message

The journey back to the Sleeping Dragon Inn took two days. The Raven grew distant, troubled. Several times she clutched her head, as if listening to a voice only she could hear.

When she finally did speak, her tone was clipped:“The professor… he may have found a cure for Brightmarch.”

The mission became clear.

### Return to the Sleeping Dragon

Back inside the inn, the party sought out Geffal. He had a message waiting and immediately confronted them about the plague spreading through Brightmarch. He demanded answers about the gelatinous cube incident, the professor’s research, and the horrors beneath the city.

Ace loudly claimed they knew nothing.

Kaelthar corrected this lie with a single, harsh gesture. Ace, humiliated beyond measure, fled the room as the truth spilled out.

Geffal’s expression hardened.“You must go. Help the professor. Save Brightmarch.”

### Toward the Dying City

After two days of preparation and travel, the party reached the outskirts of Brightmarch once more. They descended into the canal network—where the water ran sickly and dark, and where the first signs of the plague festered.

Ahead lay a city cracking beneath panic, sickness, and whatever horrors the professor had uncovered.

Tomorrow, they would enter Brightmarch again—not to destroy it…but to save it.

## Session 8 - 11/17/25

### Into the Dying City

### Into Brightmarch Once More

Under cover of darkness, the party slipped back into Brightmarch through the northern canals, guided by Geffal and his knowledge of the city’s hidden paths. The once-holy waterways were choked with filth and fear, their waters sluggish and foul.

Kaelthar sent his owl familiar ahead, silently scouting and drawing the attention of wandering undead. Thanks to the distraction, the party moved through the canals with only a handful of tense encounters, avoiding outright disaster as groans echoed through the stone corridors.

### The Professor’s Cure

At last, they reached Professor Elric Vaunt’s home. Time was precious. Vaunt revealed the fruits of his work: a functioning cure sprayer backpack and the blueprints needed to replicate the cure on a larger scale.

But he could not do it alone.

If Brightmarch was to be saved, the cure would need to be mass-produced. For that, the party would have to go to the Artificers’ Guild.

### The Artificers’ Guild

Within the guild halls, the party met Rhyla Copperbrace, who listened carefully as Vaunt’s designs were explained. After a brief but intense deliberation, Rhyla and several younger artificers agreed to help.

They revealed a troubling truth: the misting device capable of dispersing the cure through the air had originally been commissioned by the God-King himself. The artificers had refused to complete it, fearing what he might use it for. Now, that same device would be turned against his designs.

As Rhyla and her apprentices worked tirelessly to duplicate the cure and install it into the misting system, the party waited.

### While the Cure Was Forged

During the long hours in the guild:

Ace secured an unusual enchantment for his glaive — Cat Calling, allowing him to attract nearby cats once per day.

Ranger spoke with the forge master, requesting that the inquisitor’s golden mask be reforged into a wearable piece bearing the sigil of House Cindralis.

Kaelthar searched for healing potions but found every available hand committed to producing the cure.

Evander slept.

Elyndor, observing the forge work alongside Ranger, requested a metal quarterstaff and was promptly offered a long circular bar of scrap metal in response.

Eventually, the work was complete.

### Escape Across the Rooftops

The party departed the Artificers’ Guild with an additional cure sprayer backpack, moving swiftly across Brightmarch’s rooftops to avoid patrols. Geffal remained behind, choosing to stand with his fellow artificers as the cure began to spread through the city.

### Biigs and the Truth of Nullite

On the outskirts of Brightmarch, the party encountered Biigs once more. In his strange, meandering way, he spoke of Nullite — an anti-identity material that suppresses or erases the self, found most often in the Pit and in rare places throughout the world.

He also confirmed what the party feared.

Hammond was in the Pit.

### The Raven’s Judgment

The party returned to the Sleeping Dragon Inn to find Seraphine Corvallen interrogating captured Marshals. When they told her what Biigs had said, her decision was swift and merciless.

She slit the prisoners’ throats without hesitation.

Seraphine warned them then: Biigs was something else entirely — almost a cosmic entity. The God-King claimed he was a devil, but she did not believe that lie.

The truth, whatever it was, ran far deeper.

### Names Unmasked

Before leaving the city behind, truths long carried in silence finally surfaced.

Ranger revealed his real name: Rellim Cindralis, a distant heir to the fallen Kingdom of Irillis. Elyndor followed, admitting that he too carried the blood of Cindralis in his lineage.

The weight of history settled heavily over the group.

Ace broke the moment with a crooked grin and a laugh.“Yeah—and I’m an orc.”

The tension cracked, if only for a breath.

### Toward the Pit

With the cure deployed, Brightmarch given a chance to survive, and Hammond’s location finally known, the path ahead was clear.

Next, the party would travel to Ironwell. Next, they would descend into the Pit.

## Session 9 - 12/6/25

### Descent Into the Pit

*(9-hour in-person session)*

### The Road to Ironwell

At dawn, the party was roused from their rest beneath the Sleeping Dragon Inn by Seraphine Corvallen. Her voice was calm, sharp, and final. It was time to move. Time to save Hammond.

Before they departed, Bjork slipped a folded note into Kaelthar’s hand. Its contents were not shared immediately, but the reaction was unmistakable. Once on the road, Kaelthar revealed its meaning: Geffal, acting swiftly after the party’s departure from the Artificers’ Guild, had sent a shipment of supplies along with detailed instructions for brewing healing potions. Knowledge meant to preserve life. The party took note.

Ironwell lay two days away.

By midday of the first day, the road split. At the fork sat a gaunt hermit named Jax, slumped against a rotting signpost. He warned them away from the eastern road.

“Mellowfen is cursed.”

For over twenty years, villagers had vanished at night—not dragged away, not slain, but called. Voices of dead loved ones whispered from the dark, luring the living to follow. Those who listened were never seen again. Jax spoke of his wife and daughter, both lost to the curse, and of a town that had never recovered.

Despite fear and reason alike, the party turned east.

### Mellowfen and the Well

Mellowfen greeted them in silence. Lanterns burned dimly. Doors were barred. Windows shuttered tight. As night fell, the mayor rushed them inside, trembling as he explained the curse had persisted for two decades. Always the same. Always at night. Always voices.

They all led to the well in the center of town.

A plan was formed. Ace, Kaelthar, and Ranger approached the well under cover of darkness. The air around it felt wrong—heavy, cold, waiting. Whispering voices rose from its depths. At first indistinct. Then personal.

Ranger heard his father’s voice.

Instinct overcame thought. The rope was cut. The three plunged into darkness.

They landed hard in a cavern littered with bones—some ancient, some heartbreakingly small. Hovering above them was the spectral form of a young girl. She asked for her mother. Then her voice twisted with rage.

“She pushed me. Kill mommy.”

The rest of the party descended quickly. Kaelthar, seeing the truth in the mingled remains, acted without hesitation. Arcane fire obliterated the larger pile of bones. The shade screamed as it unraveled, the curse finally broken.

The truth emerged: Jax’s wife had pushed their daughter into the well, then followed her. The child’s rage had bound her spirit, bleeding outward into Mellowfen.

The party found raw blue crystal deposits embedded in the cavern walls—condensed blue dust, unknown to the villagers above.

They returned the child’s remains to the mayor and later to Jax himself. He did not cry. He only nodded.

Some curses do not end in triumph. Only in closure.

At first light, they left Mellowfen behind and turned once more toward Ironwell.

### The Pit of Ironwell

The Pit rose before them like a wound in the earth—massive, industrial, merciless. A circular prison shaft nearly a thousand feet deep, capped with heavy metal plates, cranes, guard towers, and slave lifts.

The party observed for a full day, learning guard rotations and routines. As night fell, they moved.

Seraphine remained behind on the rocky cliffs. The closer they came to the Pit, the more she seemed to lose herself—her identity fraying under its weight. Evander, notably, felt no such effect.

The party split.

Kaelthar and Ranger infiltrated the watchtower, subduing the guard after a brief struggle.

Ace, Elyndor, and Evander ambushed a guard outside the guardhouse.

When the moment was right, the party stormed the guardhouse itself, capturing the remaining guards while they were drunk and unsuspecting.

Interrogation revealed little. None of the guards had ever been below. None truly understood what lay beneath them.

### The Descent Begins

The party entered The Maw, the upper levels of the Pit. Storage rooms lined the walls. Gears and control booths dominated the north. Slave lifts waited on the eastern edge.

Before descending further, Evander kicked one of the captured Marshals into the center of the Pit. His screams echoed for a long time… then faded.

They descended into The Warrens.

### The Survivor

The moment they arrived, whispers spread among the prisoners.

“The survivor.”

Crowds pressed toward Evander, grasping at him, begging.

“Save us.”

The unrest drew attention. Elven thralls with nullite spikes driven through their eyes screeched and charged. More poured from the tunnels. The party fought briefly, then fled deeper into the Pit, dragging the remaining prisoners with them.

### The Dominion Echo

Seven hundred feet down, they entered The Chasm—a fractured cavern glowing faintly with violet-blue light. This was a Dominion Echo, a remnant of the Seraph’s aspect of command and will. It recognized Evander. As the man it lended its power to all those years ago.

All others felt crushing pressure. All but Ace failed to resist it, collapsing to their knees, unable to move or speak.

Evander approached the glowing fracture.

A voice spoke in his mind:

“Ahhh you have followed our will well… Others follow others’ wills. Radiance whispers. Awakening slumbers. Eternity is dead. DOMINION MUST LIVE.”

Power flooded him. Purple mist coiled around his body. He did not resist.

Evander leapt across the Pit and attacked his own party. Ace alone held him back, protecting the others until he landed a blow strong enough to break the effect.

Evander collapsed.

When he awoke, something remained. The Voice of Dominion had marked him.

### The Abyssal Floor

The party descended into the lowest depths of the Pit.

There, suspended in a cage fifty feet above the floor, was Hammond—unconscious, unmoving.

The Warden spoke.

He had been waiting.

As he taunted them, Kaelthar warped Hammond’s body onto the lift. The party pulled the lever and began ascending immediately, trying to escape without engaging.

The Warden pursued, halting the lift with his whip.

The battle raged atop the rising platform. Blow after blow was exchanged. The Warden focused his fury on Ace—but Ace endured.

In the end, Ace shoved the Warden from the lift.

The Warden fell.

## Session 10 - 12/21/25

### The Weight of the Pit

### The Price of Dominion

### Ash and Aftermath

The party began their search on the Abyssal Floor, moving through scorched stone, drifting ash, and the lingering heat of the Nullite forge. The Warden’s dragonborn body lay broken where it had fallen. Kaelthar and Ranger examined the corpse and discovered something deeply unsettling: despite the shattered armor and torn chest, a faint purple glow still pulsed within the old wounds, as though something inside refused to fully die.

While this investigation continued, Ace and Evander pressed deeper into the Nullite Forge itself. There they discovered a hidden chamber containing forgotten notes, strange markings, and a sealed chest — evidence that the forge’s purpose extended far beyond simple labor or punishment. On their return, the dragonborn’s body began to sink into the molten flow of the forge, flesh and bone dissolving as if claimed by the Pit itself. From the ruin of his chest, a crystalline mass forced its way free and slipped into the lava below.

### The Crystal Dragon

Moments later, the crystal erupted.

A massive purple dragon burst forth from the molten river, screaming as it emerged into the chamber. The shockwave hurled Evander across the stone. Chaos followed instantly. Ranger forced Hammond onto the elevator and sent him upward, desperate to get him out of the forge before everything collapsed. Attempts to strike the crystal core failed as heat and smoke obscured the battlefield.

Kaelthar unleashed frost, ice crawling across the crystal’s surface. For a moment, it shrank — then pulsed — and returned to its full, terrible size.

Evander rose from the stone, bloodied but unbroken.

He reached out.

And dominated it.

The dragon bowed to his will.

### The Bargain of Dominion

In that moment of absolute control, Dominion answered.

Not as a voice shouted across the cavern, but as a presence — vast, inevitable, and patient. Dominion offered Evander a way forward: exodus from the Pit, not just for himself, but for those bound within it. An escape no lift, no rope, no army could ever achieve.

But nothing was given freely.

In exchange, Dominion claimed a Right of Request.

At some unknowable point in the future, Dominion would call upon Evander for one single act. One command. One obedience. The nature of the request was undefined — it could be small or world-shattering, merciful or monstrous — but it would be absolute. When the time came, Evander would not be allowed to refuse.

The choice was clear.

Leave the Pit now, abandoning those still trapped…or accept a debt written not in blood, but in fate itself.

Evander accepted.

The forge trembled as the pact sealed. Dominion’s will settled deeper into him — not louder, not crueler, but heavier. Patient. Waiting.

### Chains Broken

With the immediate threat subdued, the party ascended to the second level of the Pit. There, Evander’s growing power revealed its full weight. He commanded the enslaved elves bound by Nullite — and they obeyed instantly, their movements no longer driven by pain or coercion.

It was there that Ranger found him.

An elf lay dying among the others, blood running from his nose, breath shallow and uneven. Something about him felt wrong — familiar. As Ranger knelt beside him, the elf seized his head, and the world shattered.

Memories poured forth: open fields, ruined stone, wind through tall grass, and a quiet man teaching a child how to draw a bow.

The truth was unmistakable.

It was Ranger’s father.

Ranger lifted him into his arms and carried him to the elevator, grief heavy enough to bend the air around him.

### The Bargain Fulfilled

As they reached the first level, Kaelthar finally managed to send word to Seraphine Corvallen. Evander commanded one of the thralls to silence the alarm, then entered communion once more with Dominion.

When he returned, his resolve was absolute.

He told the party that escape would be granted — but the Pit would not release him forever. One day, Dominion would call him back. When that day came, Evander would return… and become the next Warden.

The party agreed. There was no other choice.

But Evander was not finished.

Before the pact could close, he issued one final command: every prisoner would be freed. Not only the miners and slaves — but the elven thralls as well.

The world went black.

### The Freed and the Fallen

When consciousness returned, the party lay beneath open sky in a forest clearing. Around them stood hundreds of survivors, freed in an instant by the will of Dominion.

The elves stood among them — but the Nullite had vanished from their eyes.

So had their lives.

They fell where they stood, finally at rest.

### Camp of the Freed

Lost but alive, the party gathered the survivors beneath the forest canopy. Kaelthar’s owl confirmed Ebonmere to the east, but no city could shelter so many — and no city could protect them from what would surely hunt them now.

The freed made their choice.

They would follow.

That night, the camp burned with quiet fires and quieter grief. Hundreds slept uneasy under the stars — liberated, hunted, and bound together by fate.

And somewhere deep within Evander, Dominion waited.

## Session 11 - 1/12/26

### The Weight of the Living

You wake beneath an open sky.

Smoke curls from dying embers as hundreds of refugees stir among the trees — former prisoners, miners, slaves, and the broken remnants of the Pit. Some weep quietly. Others stare into the forest, afraid to sleep again.

Hammond lies nearby, alive but weak. Ironwell smolders somewhere far behind you. And the God-King now knows something impossible has happened.

Evander stands apart from the camp, silent — carrying a destiny he cannot escape. Ranger feels the weight of blood and memory like a blade pressed against his spine. Kaelthar’s magic hums uneasily, as if the world itself has noticed the balance shift.

You have saved hundreds.

Now you must decide how to protect them.

The forest is vast. The enemy is relentless. And dawn is coming.

### A Foothold in the Dark

### The Caves of Refuge

Morning broke over the forest camp in uneasy quiet. Prisoners stirred. Fires smoldered low. Before orders were spoken, movement began—Ace drifting toward the nearby cave system, restless and eager. The party followed, understanding the unspoken goal: if the rescued prisoners were to survive, they needed shelter. Somewhere defensible. Somewhere hidden.

As they approached the cave mouth, Rellim cloaked the group in shadow, weaving magic to veil their presence and allow for quiet scouting. The illusion held—until it didn’t.

The clash of armor against stone echoed through the cavern.

The ground trembled.

Without warning, an umberhulk burst from the earth, its massive mandibles clamping down on Evander’s direwolf before it could react. As the creature attempted to retreat back into the stone, Kaelthar lashed out with his whip, hauling the thrashing monstrosity back into the open.

Ace didn’t hesitate.

He brought his hammer down with crushing force, shattering the umberhulk’s armored head and splintering its chitin like brittle shell.

### The Swarm Beneath

Expecting more, Ace hammered his weapon against the cavern wall, roaring a challenge into the depths. The earth answered.

Two more umberhulks erupted from the stone, and the cave path became a brutal melee. Blows were traded in tight quarters as the creatures surged forward. One fell beneath Ace’s relentless assault, slammed so hard it was driven into the ceiling stone.

The third attempted to flee.

Ace followed it—leaping into its burrow without hesitation.

Moments later, the ground shook violently. The tunnel collapsed inward, sealing the deeper passages and stranding Elyndor on the far side of the rubble.

### The Broodmother Revealed

With the path blocked, Evander tended to his wounds and to his injured wolf while the others searched desperately for another way through. Elyndor pressed onward alone—until he blinked back through a narrow crack in the rubble, pale and breathless.

He had seen something worse.

An umberhulk queen, heavy with brood, deeper within the caves.

Exhausted and bloodied, the party chose to rest before pressing further. Kaelthar conjured a protective arcane barrier, ensuring the group could recover without fear of another ambush.

### The Queen’s Chamber

When they rose, Kaelthar cleared the collapsed path with a roaring fireball, opening the way to the heart of the caves. Preparations were made carefully this time.

Rellim moved ahead in silence. Ace taunted from the front. Steel and spell readied for the final fight.

The broodmother did not fall easily.

Its chitin resisted blade and hammer alike. Terror radiated from it, shaking half the party to their core. The creature’s presence twisted minds and wills—forcing Kaelthar, against his own control, to destroy his familiar in a moment of horrifying compulsion.

Still, the party pressed on.

Ace began to glow with power, illuminating the chamber as he drove the assault forward. Slowly, relentlessly, the queen weakened—until Elyndor slid beneath its massive frame and split its egg sac open, delivering the killing blow.

### A Place to Stand

The broodmother’s death scream shook the cavern. Stone rained down from above.

A boulder crashed toward Rellim and Kaelthar. Rellim dove clear. Kaelthar was pinned beneath the weight of it, crushed and bleeding—until Rellim dragged him free and tended his wounds.

As the dust settled, the party dissected the corpse, harvesting its mandibles. Beyond the chamber, they discovered something unexpected: a larger cavern, open to the sky. Sunlight poured through the broken roof. Fresh water flowed freely across stone worn smooth by time.

A refuge.

A defensible haven.

A place where the freed prisoners could rest, recover, and begin again.

Here, beneath open sky and stone alike, the party established their foothold. Here, their growing resistance took root. Here, they prepared for the long war still to come.

The God-King’s shadow had not been broken.

But it had been challenged.

## Session 12 - 2/4/26

### Divided Paths

### Building a Refuge

With the umberhulks slain and the cavern secured, the party began transforming the underground chamber into a refuge for the hundreds of prisoners they had freed from the Pit.

Evander led groups of refugees in harvesting the fallen umberhulk carcasses. The work was unpleasant, the meat thick and rubbery and nearly tasteless—but it was food, and food meant survival.

Nearby, Kaelthar gathered refugees and began constructing makeshift sleeping spaces. Branches, leaves, and scavenged debris were shaped into crude cots so that the exhausted survivors could finally rest somewhere safer than the bare stone.

When the meat was prepared, Kaelthar and Evander combined their magic to cure and flavor the otherwise foul umberhulk flesh, transforming the meal into something closer to seasoned game. The simple act lifted spirits immediately. For the first time since escaping the Pit, laughter returned to the cavern.

Elsewhere, Rellim quietly spoke with Elyndor about his celestial patron. Elyndor revealed that his patron claimed to serve the good of the realm—but had warned him to watch Evander carefully. No accusations were made, but the warning lingered in the air like distant thunder.

Before leaving the refuge, the party checked on Hammond. The scars of the Pit still clung to him, and his mind wandered like a lantern in the wind. Yet he was improving slowly, each day reclaiming a little more of himself.

### The Cave’s True Owner

Just as preparations to travel began, a strange visitor descended from the cavern ceiling.

A small blue fairy, wings humming like glass chimes, appeared suddenly and demanded to know why the party had taken up residence in his cave.

The fairy introduced himself as Morvath, explaining that he had been living in the cavern for some time after somehow becoming stranded outside the Feywild. Since then he had survived by scavenging and occasionally visiting taverns on the outskirts of civilization.

After a tense exchange, the party struck a deal.

Morvath wished to travel to Ebonmere, hoping to purchase land somewhere in Aurelion and repay a kindness once shown to him by a young girl. In return for passage and assistance, he agreed to allow the party to use the cavern as a permanent refuge for their growing resistance.

With the terms agreed upon, the group set out toward Ebonmere.

### Shadow Over the Road

During the journey, the party’s path was darkened by the passing shadow of a massive bronze dragon. The creature soared overhead, blotting out the sky as it flew north toward Silverhold.

The group quickly hid beneath the trees.

Fortunately, the dragon did not stop.

Once the thunder of its wings faded, the party continued their journey toward the city.

### Two Paths into the City

Upon reaching Ebonmere, the party divided their efforts.

Kaelthar and Rellim would enter the Inner Ring of the city.

Ace, Evander, Elyndor, and Morvath would remain in the slums and outskirts, gathering supplies and resources for the refugees hiding in the wilderness.

Each group had a different role to play in strengthening their growing resistance.

### The Inner Ring — A Return Home

To pass the city gates, Rellim adopted the identity of Percival Fitzgerald III, posing as a wealthy traveler escorting Kaelthar on scholarly business.

The ruse worked. The guards allowed them through, though not without a warning to Kaelthar to keep his “navigator” under control inside the city.

Their first destination was Kaelthar’s family estate.

Outside the home, Kaelthar was greeted by an old and crotchety neighbor who had not seen him since he was a boy. From the neighbor, they learned that Kaelthar’s mentor Veythar had last visited the estate roughly a year ago, but the house had otherwise remained untouched.

Inside, the estate was dusty and quiet.

They first explored Kaelthar’s childhood room, which bore signs that someone had lived there recently—likely Veythar himself. It seemed the mentor had chosen to stay in the young mage’s room, leaving the rest of the house undisturbed.

Then Kaelthar opened the door to his parents’ room.

The air was thick with memory. Years had passed since anyone entered that room—not even Kaelthar’s father had disturbed it after the tragedy that occurred there. After only a moment, the door was closed again.

Finally, they entered Kronos Chronozar’s study.

What had once been a laboratory of magical devices, scrolls, and experimental apparatus had been replaced with an ordinary study filled with mundane books.

All but one object remained.

A clock mounted on the wall, glowing faintly with chronurgy runes, pulsing the same blue light as Kaelthar’s hourglass.

When Kaelthar held his hourglass to the clock and channeled his magic, a hidden mechanism clicked open. Inside the clock was a secret compartment containing three empty cutouts:

A dragon scale

A coin

A tear-shaped gem

The meaning of the puzzle remained unclear, but Kaelthar carefully noted the discovery before closing the mechanism once again.

### The Ebon Bank

From the estate, the pair traveled to the Ebon Bank, a place of towering ceilings, gold-veined pillars, and the unmistakable wealth of Ebonmere’s ruling houses.

Records showed that much of the Chronozar family fortune had slowly dwindled over the years maintaining the estate. Still, enough remained to aid the party’s cause.

Attempting to withdraw the funds nearly forced Kaelthar into an uncomfortable situation: to access the account fully would require meeting with an Obligator, an official tied closely to the God-King’s bureaucracy.

Through quick thinking and careful deception, the pair managed to secure their gold without revealing too much—and quickly left before drawing further attention.

### News of the City

Before leaving the district, they stopped at a public bulletin board where several notices were posted:

Announcements for the Victory Pits arena festival

A new Marshal Chief appointed within the city

Bounties for troll extermination

Various shop advertisements and propaganda

A recent article from the Ebonmere Chronicle

Each notice hinted at opportunities—or dangers—within the city.

### The Whimsical Willow

Their final stop was the Whimsical Willow, the magical shop of Kaelthar’s old family friend Otto.

The shop hummed with enchantment, arcane energy lingering in the air like insects buzzing in summer heat. Otto himself moved through the store aided by magical duplicates of himself, allowing him to assist multiple customers at once.

There, Kaelthar and Rellim acquired several useful magical trinkets to aid the party in the difficult days ahead.

### The Slums — Supplies and Bloodsport

While Kaelthar and Rellim navigated the Inner Ring, the rest of the party worked the streets of Ebonmere’s lower districts.

Evander sold the harvested umberhulk remains to a local shopkeeper, with plans to return later for potions crafted from the creature’s strange biology.

Ace and Elyndor turned their attention to securing supplies for the refugees. Elyndor negotiated with quiet precision while Ace spun a convincing story about supplies destined for an orphanage. The merchant, moved by the tale, provided an impressive haul of goods free of charge—unaware the supplies would instead feed a hidden resistance.

With food and tools secured, the group followed Morvath to an underground bare-knuckle fighting pit.

Morvath and Ace entered the ring.

Morvath fell quickly.

Ace did not.

Facing two opponents alone, he endured the assault, dropped the larger fighter, and finished the second with a brutal strike that sent the crowd roaring.

By the end of the night, the group walked away battered, bruised—and 450 gold richer.

### A Resistance Takes Root

While Ebonmere carried on with its normal life—markets bustling, nobles scheming, and the God-King’s rule looming overhead—something new was beginning beyond its walls.

In a hidden cavern, hundreds of freed prisoners now lived because of the party’s actions.

Food had been secured. Coin had been earned. Allies had been found.

The resistance was no longer an idea.

It was becoming something real.

## Session 13 - 2/18/26

### Secrets of the City and the Woods

### Errands in the Inner Ring

While the resistance gathered strength beyond the walls of Ebonmere, Kaelthar and Rellim continued their quiet work inside the city’s inner ring.
