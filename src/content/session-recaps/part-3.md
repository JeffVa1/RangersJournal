# Part III

## Session 12 - 2/4/26

### Divided Paths

### Building a Refuge

With the umberhulks slain and the cavern secured, the party began transforming the underground chamber into a refuge for the hundreds of prisoners they had freed from the Pit.

Evander led groups of refugees in harvesting the fallen umberhulk carcasses. The work was unpleasant, the meat thick and rubbery and nearly tasteless—but it was food, and food meant survival.

Nearby, Kaelthar gathered refugees and began constructing makeshift sleeping spaces. Branches, leaves, and scavenged debris were shaped into crude cots so that the exhausted survivors could finally rest somewhere safer than the bare stone.

When the meat was prepared, Kaelthar and Evander combined their magic to cure and flavor the otherwise foul umberhulk flesh, transforming the meal into something closer to seasoned game. The simple act lifted spirits immediately. For the first time since escaping the Pit, laughter returned to the cavern.

Elsewhere, Rellim quietly spoke with Elyndor about his celestial patron. Elyndor revealed that his patron claimed to serve the good of the realm—but had warned him to watch Evander carefully. No accusations were made, but the warning lingered in the air like distant thunder.

Before leaving the refuge, the party checked on Hammond. The scars of the Pit still clung to him, and his mind wandered like a lantern in the wind. Yet he was improving slowly, each day reclaiming a little more of himself.

### The Cave’s True Owner

Just as preparations to travel began, a strange visitor descended from the cavern ceiling.

A small blue fairy, wings humming like glass chimes, appeared suddenly and demanded to know why the party had taken up residence in his cave.

The fairy introduced himself as Morvath, explaining that he had been living in the cavern for some time after somehow becoming stranded outside the Feywild. Since then he had survived by scavenging and occasionally visiting taverns on the outskirts of civilization.

After a tense exchange, the party struck a deal.

Morvath wished to travel to Ebonmere, hoping to purchase land somewhere in Aurelion and repay a kindness once shown to him by a young girl. In return for passage and assistance, he agreed to allow the party to use the cavern as a permanent refuge for their growing resistance.

With the terms agreed upon, the group set out toward Ebonmere.

### Shadow Over the Road

During the journey, the party’s path was darkened by the passing shadow of a massive bronze dragon. The creature soared overhead, blotting out the sky as it flew north toward Silverhold.

The group quickly hid beneath the trees.

Fortunately, the dragon did not stop.

Once the thunder of its wings faded, the party continued their journey toward the city.

### Two Paths into the City

Upon reaching Ebonmere, the party divided their efforts.

Kaelthar and Rellim would enter the Inner Ring of the city.

Ace, Evander, Elyndor, and Morvath would remain in the slums and outskirts, gathering supplies and resources for the refugees hiding in the wilderness.

Each group had a different role to play in strengthening their growing resistance.

### The Inner Ring — A Return Home

To pass the city gates, Rellim adopted the identity of Percival Fitzgerald III, posing as a wealthy traveler escorting Kaelthar on scholarly business.

The ruse worked. The guards allowed them through, though not without a warning to Kaelthar to keep his “navigator” under control inside the city.

Their first destination was Kaelthar’s family estate.

Outside the home, Kaelthar was greeted by an old and crotchety neighbor who had not seen him since he was a boy. From the neighbor, they learned that Kaelthar’s mentor Veythar had last visited the estate roughly a year ago, but the house had otherwise remained untouched.

Inside, the estate was dusty and quiet.

They first explored Kaelthar’s childhood room, which bore signs that someone had lived there recently—likely Veythar himself. It seemed the mentor had chosen to stay in the young mage’s room, leaving the rest of the house undisturbed.

Then Kaelthar opened the door to his parents’ room.

The air was thick with memory. Years had passed since anyone entered that room—not even Kaelthar’s father had disturbed it after the tragedy that occurred there. After only a moment, the door was closed again.

Finally, they entered Kronos Chronozar’s study.

What had once been a laboratory of magical devices, scrolls, and experimental apparatus had been replaced with an ordinary study filled with mundane books.

All but one object remained.

A clock mounted on the wall, glowing faintly with chronurgy runes, pulsing the same blue light as Kaelthar’s hourglass.

When Kaelthar held his hourglass to the clock and channeled his magic, a hidden mechanism clicked open. Inside the clock was a secret compartment containing three empty cutouts:

A dragon scale

A coin

A tear-shaped gem

The meaning of the puzzle remained unclear, but Kaelthar carefully noted the discovery before closing the mechanism once again.

### The Ebon Bank

From the estate, the pair traveled to the Ebon Bank, a place of towering ceilings, gold-veined pillars, and the unmistakable wealth of Ebonmere’s ruling houses.

Records showed that much of the Chronozar family fortune had slowly dwindled over the years maintaining the estate. Still, enough remained to aid the party’s cause.

Attempting to withdraw the funds nearly forced Kaelthar into an uncomfortable situation: to access the account fully would require meeting with an Obligator, an official tied closely to the God-King’s bureaucracy.

Through quick thinking and careful deception, the pair managed to secure their gold without revealing too much—and quickly left before drawing further attention.

### News of the City

Before leaving the district, they stopped at a public bulletin board where several notices were posted:

Announcements for the Victory Pits arena festival

A new Marshal Chief appointed within the city

Bounties for troll extermination

Various shop advertisements and propaganda

A recent article from the Ebonmere Chronicle

Each notice hinted at opportunities—or dangers—within the city.

### The Whimsical Willow

Their final stop was the Whimsical Willow, the magical shop of Kaelthar’s old family friend Otto.

The shop hummed with enchantment, arcane energy lingering in the air like insects buzzing in summer heat. Otto himself moved through the store aided by magical duplicates of himself, allowing him to assist multiple customers at once.

There, Kaelthar and Rellim acquired several useful magical trinkets to aid the party in the difficult days ahead.

### The Slums — Supplies and Bloodsport

While Kaelthar and Rellim navigated the Inner Ring, the rest of the party worked the streets of Ebonmere’s lower districts.

Evander sold the harvested umberhulk remains to a local shopkeeper, with plans to return later for potions crafted from the creature’s strange biology.

Ace and Elyndor turned their attention to securing supplies for the refugees. Elyndor negotiated with quiet precision while Ace spun a convincing story about supplies destined for an orphanage. The merchant, moved by the tale, provided an impressive haul of goods free of charge—unaware the supplies would instead feed a hidden resistance.

With food and tools secured, the group followed Morvath to an underground bare-knuckle fighting pit.

Morvath and Ace entered the ring.

Morvath fell quickly.

Ace did not.

Facing two opponents alone, he endured the assault, dropped the larger fighter, and finished the second with a brutal strike that sent the crowd roaring.

By the end of the night, the group walked away battered, bruised—and 450 gold richer.

### A Resistance Takes Root

While Ebonmere carried on with its normal life—markets bustling, nobles scheming, and the God-King’s rule looming overhead—something new was beginning beyond its walls.

In a hidden cavern, hundreds of freed prisoners now lived because of the party’s actions.

Food had been secured. Coin had been earned. Allies had been found.

The resistance was no longer an idea.

It was becoming something real.

## Session 13 - 2/18/26

### Secrets of the City and the Woods

### Errands in the Inner Ring

While the resistance gathered strength beyond the walls of Ebonmere, Kaelthar and Rellim continued their quiet work inside the city’s inner ring.

Their first stop was The Simple Tonic, a small herbalist shop tucked along one of the quieter streets. Inside they met Clara Navelle, an older blue dragonborn whose knowledge of herbs and alchemy seemed as sharp as the scent of dried plants that filled the shop. Rellim purchased a guide to the plants of the Godswood, while Kaelthar acquired several potions that might prove useful in the trials ahead.

From there they traveled to The Quiet Current, a courier house near the city gates. Its keeper, a green dragonborn named Janice Rillian, insisted that every messenger was booked for two full days. Coin—and a small gemstone—changed her mind. A message was sent immediately.

While there, the pair secured two pairs of sending stones so they could remain in contact with the rest of the party, and another pair intended for Geffal, their ally beyond the city.

### The Secret of the Study

That evening they returned to Kaelthar’s estate, where the quiet halls held more than just memories.

The following morning Kaelthar resumed searching his father’s study while Rellim explored the estate library. There he uncovered a history of the great cities that read suspiciously like propaganda, along with a bestiary of Aurelion.

Among the papers scattered across the desk they discovered a note left by Veythar, Kaelthar’s mentor.

At first glance it appeared sloppy and careless, riddled with strange phrasing and misspellings. But upon closer inspection the wording revealed itself to be intentional. Hidden within the text were instructions for activating a mechanism concealed within the study.

Three items were required:

A dragon scale

A tear-shaped vial of liquid

A coin marked KM

Kaelthar placed a scale from his own tail into the first slot. Rellim poured a potion into the hollow shaped like a tear. The final piece—the KM coin—slid into place beside them.

When Kaelthar lifted his chronurgy hourglass and channeled his magic through it, time itself seemed to hesitate.

With a quiet click, the desk opened.

Inside lay a scroll case etched with glowing blue sigils, clearly enchanted so that its message would exist only briefly once revealed.

Kaelthar opened it while Rellim steadied him. The scroll appeared for only moments before dissolving into nothing—but not before Kaelthar managed to copy its contents.

The message was from Veythar.

It spoke of Kronos Chronozar’s work, of dangerous rumors resurfacing, and of old equations and symbols appearing once more across the world. Veythar revealed that he had left for Thalmyre, seeking answers.

And perhaps the most unsettling truth of all:

Kronos Chronozar might not be as lost as everyone believed.

### Rumors of Trolls

Seeking more information about the dangers surrounding Ebonmere, Kaelthar and Rellim visited the Marshal’s Office.

There they learned that trolls had recently been spotted in the Godswood northwest of the city, and that a bounty of two hundred gold per troll had been issued. Two had already been slain—but it had taken nearly thirty slumfolk to accomplish the task.

The forest beyond the city walls was becoming increasingly dangerous.

### The Missing Girl

While Kaelthar and Rellim conducted their investigations, the rest of the party followed another lead within the slums.

A young girl named Clara had gone missing.

When Ace, Evander, Elyndor, and Morvath arrived at the girl’s home, they found her parents speaking with two Marshals. The moment Morvath appeared, the girl’s mother panicked—accusing the fairy of taking her daughter.

Evander and Ace quickly seized Morvath in an attempt to appear helpful rather than suspicious, while the Marshals pressed them with questions. Though the exchange grew tense, the party ultimately offered their help in searching for the girl.

The parents explained that Clara had gone into the Godswood, wearing a brown cloak and tunic.

The Marshals allowed the party to assist—but warned them to keep a close watch on Morvath.

### Into the Godswood

The party eventually regrouped and began searching the forest.

Near the edge of the Godswood, Ace and Elyndor spotted a faint footprint in the soil. When Rellim examined it more closely, he confirmed it belonged to a young girl, and the trail could not have been more than two days old.

Rellim took the lead, tracking Clara’s path deeper into the woods.

Hours passed beneath the forest canopy.

Then the silence broke.

### Trolls in the Clearing

Nearly five hours into the search, the party emerged into a clearing where two trolls lumbered among the trees.

They struck quickly.

Ace charged first, slamming both hammers into the troll’s legs and drawing a furious roar. Elyndor followed with a blast of eldritch energy that struck the creature squarely in the chest. Evander, momentarily shaken by the creature’s size, missed his first attack but recovered quickly—launching a second strike wreathed in fire.

His dire wolf lunged at the second troll, keeping it distracted.

Kaelthar’s magic struck the wounded troll with devastating force, obliterating its head in a burst of arcane power. Morvath descended from above, unleashing his own magic upon the remaining beast.

Rellim attempted to fire—but the snap of a twig beneath his boot startled the troll, spoiling the shot.

Ace ended the fight moments later, smashing the creature’s knees and dropping it to the forest floor.

### A Darker Influence

While examining the bodies, Rellim noticed something strange.

At the base of one troll’s neck, a faint purple glow pulsed beneath the skin.

When Kaelthar studied it more closely, the magic felt disturbingly familiar—ancient, fading, but unmistakable.

Something connected to Dominion…or perhaps the influence of an Inquisitor.

Whatever force had touched the troll, it was not natural.

And it meant something far more dangerous might be moving through the Godswood.

With that uneasy discovery lingering in their minds, the party pressed deeper into the forest, continuing their search for the missing girl.

## Session 14 - 2/25/26

### The Hag’s Game

### The Trail Grows Darker

After the battle with the first pair of trolls, the party lingered briefly among the fallen creatures. Rellim knelt beside the bodies, carefully draining a portion of their dark blood into a flask. Ancient texts had once suggested that troll ichor, when treated properly, could serve in powerful restorative concoctions. Whether truth or academic speculation, he intended to find out.

Soon after, the trail of the missing girl, Clara, was found again—but it no longer ran alone. Alongside her lighter footprints were the deep gouges of troll tracks, clawed impressions cutting through the forest floor.

As the party pressed deeper into the Godswood, something else began watching them.

Rellim glimpsed a woman in the distance—her skin green as new leaves, her form almost blending with the forest around her. The moment he focused on her, she vanished like mist under sunlight.

The forest offered little peace afterward.

Ace unknowingly stepped into a writhing bed of snakes disguised beneath fallen leaves. His startled reaction sent the creatures scattering into the undergrowth. When Rellim looked again into the trees, the strange woman was there once more, smiling faintly from behind a trunk.

Then she disappeared again.

### The Troll Ambush

Hours later the forest opened into a clearing where six trolls wandered lazily among the trees.

The party quickly formed a plan:

Ace and Morvath flanked one side of the clearing

Elyndor and Evander, with Evander’s dire wolf, took the opposite side

Kaelthar and Rellim climbed a ridge overlooking the trolls

When all were in place, Kaelthar cast a minor illusion, drawing one of the trolls toward the phantom.

Then he unleashed a fireball.

The explosion engulfed four trolls in roaring flame.

Rellim followed instantly, releasing three arrows in swift succession. The first gauged the wind, while the next two struck true—piercing a troll’s eyes and pinning its skull to the earth.

Across the clearing, Elyndor and Evander struck down another stunned troll, while Ace and Morvath carved through a third in a violent clash of steel and fury.

The remaining trolls struggled to recover from the assault, but the party pressed the advantage. Fire and steel ensured the creatures would not rise again.

Soon the clearing fell silent.

The party collected troll ears as proof for the Marshal bounty and turned toward the cave hidden beyond the clearing.

They knew what waited inside.

The hag.

### The Hag’s Lair

Inside the cavern, Ace was the first to spot Clara, the missing girl.

Morvath rushed toward her without hesitation.

That was when the trap sprang.

The cavern erupted with illusion and laughter. Trolls appeared from thin air as the hag’s voice echoed through the chamber. Morvath was hurled aside and impaled upon a statue of nullite, his blood spilling across the strange metal.

Before their eyes, the statue twisted and reshaped itself into a grotesque troll bearing Morvath’s own likeness.

Battle exploded across the cavern.

A troll lunged across the bridge toward Rellim, slashing his chest and driving him backward past Kaelthar and Elyndor. Rellim recovered quickly, sending arrows toward the creature threatening Ace.

The monstrous blue Morvath-troll struck next, using Morvath’s own body as a weapon against Ace and Kaelthar before Kaelthar used magic to wrench the fairy free.

Morvath returned to the fight with burning fury, cleaving into his twisted double.

Elsewhere, Ace crushed another troll’s spine with a devastating blow, while Evander’s aura of Dominion pressed down upon the creatures, causing one troll to lash out wildly at its own ally in panic.

Kaelthar lashed the monstrous blue troll with magic, hurling it into a deep fissure below.

Morvath, enraged by the sight of the captured girl, decapitated another troll in a single brutal strike.

At last the hag herself acted.

Lightning crackled between her claws—but Kaelthar unraveled the spell before it could be cast, disrupting her magic with precise timing.

Ace rushed forward and struck the hag with his hammer, the blow ringing loudly against the nullite structure of her lair.

Rellim stepped forward and fired a poison-tipped arrow into her shoulder.

The hag only laughed.

“See you soon.”

### Into the Fey Realm

Reality shattered.

The cavern vanished in an instant.

When the party’s vision cleared, they stood beneath strange trees of impossible colors. The air shimmered with unfamiliar magic. The sky itself seemed brighter—too bright.

The forest around them was alive with unnatural beauty.

A few quiet words confirmed what their instincts already feared.

The hag had not fled.

She had moved the board.

The party now stood within the Fey Realm.

### The Hag’s Ritual

### Ambushed in the Feywild

After the hag’s magic tore the party from the Godswood and cast them into the Fey Realm, the group found themselves standing within a strange and vibrant forest. The air shimmered with unnatural life and color, the trees bending and spiraling toward a sky that seemed too bright to be real.

With no clear path back, the party moved cautiously forward.

They did not travel far before walking directly into an ambush.

At the center of a clearing lay a ritual circle, where several small frog-like creatures scurried around glowing runes while seven chanting witches raised their hands toward the sky. As the party approached, Evander rushed toward the circle—but was immediately swarmed by the creatures. Their strange magic overwhelmed him, and he collapsed into magical sleep just as the ritual neared completion.

Moments later, the air tore open.

The blue troll abomination from the hag’s lair appeared through the ritual’s magic and immediately attacked, slashing across Evander’s chest and hurling him violently into a nearby tree.

### Lightning and Fire

Elyndor quickly struck down one of the frog creatures and rushed to aid Evander. Kaelthar followed, teleporting the wounded warrior to safety and forcing a powerful healing potion down his throat.

Meanwhile, the witches continued their ritual.

Their chanting rose to a fevered pitch as lightning gathered above the circle. A single massive bolt erupted from the storm of magic, blasting across the battlefield and striking the troll, Elyndor, Rellim, Kaelthar, and Evander in a devastating line of energy.

The battle erupted into chaos.

Elyndor unleashed blasts of eldritch power against the frog creatures while Kaelthar responded with overwhelming force—casting a fireball empowered by his pearl of power directly into the cluster of witches. The explosion scattered them, and one of the witches screamed louder than the others, revealing herself as the true caster behind the ritual.

The party focused their attacks.

Yet even as wounds piled upon her, the witch vanished from sight and the ritual circle began tearing open a rift in reality.

The blue troll fought on relentlessly, refusing to fall despite the party’s attacks.

### The Hag’s Gambit

Then the hag herself appeared beside Ace.

Leaning close, she whispered a message meant only for him.

“Sister Sybil says hello.”

For a moment Ace stood frozen, staring at her. Whatever spell she had attempted failed, and panic flickered across her face.

“It didn’t work!” she shrieked as she fled.

Morvath struck at her but missed, though Ace managed to land a heavy blow with his nullite hammer. Even wounded, the hag managed to escape deeper into the clearing.

Meanwhile the troll continued its rampage.

Morvath deflected its bite as it lunged toward Ace, giving Evander the opening he needed to retaliate. In a brutal mirror of the attack that had nearly killed him moments earlier, Evander carved across the troll’s chest with savage force.

With the hag fleeing and the witches broken, the party turned their full focus on the troll.

Elyndor’s eldritch blast struck it squarely, while Kaelthar used his whip to drag the weakened witch back into the difficult terrain of the battlefield. Morvath darted forward, landing a devastating critical strike before knocking the witch unconscious moments later.

At last the troll fell.

Evander claimed its head as proof of the kill, while Rellim bound the defeated hag and handed her over to Ace for safe keeping.

### Return Through the Portal

With the ritual circle destabilizing, the party stepped through the collapsing portal and returned to the cave within the Godswood.

But their victory came at a cost.

Near the basin where the ritual had been performed, they discovered Clara’s lifeless body.

Morvath fell to his knees beside her, overcome with grief.

As the others searched the cave, Ace quickly located and looted a nearby treasure chest while Kaelthar examined a strange nullite statue nearby. Several prisoners were discovered locked deeper within the cave, and Kaelthar and Rellim began distributing supplies to help them recover.

Elyndor uncovered several mysterious rations among the hag’s supplies—though their origins were unclear.

Evander finished harvesting the troll ears from the creatures slain in the cave.

Meanwhile Kaelthar identified several magical items recovered from the chest.

### A Dark Miracle

Eventually the party returned to the ritual basin.

They placed the troll’s head upon the altar where Clara had been sacrificed.

Morvath stepped forward and cut off one of his own fingers, placing it upon the basin.

For a long moment, nothing happened.

Then Clara’s body stirred.

She gasped.

The girl lived again—but her skin had turned unnaturally blue, touched by the magic that had restored her.

The miracle was not without consequence.

Morvath immediately attempted to take Clara with him, insisting she now belonged to him in some way after the ritual.

The rest of the party reacted instantly.

Weapons were drawn.

Initiative was rolled.

And the party now faced a new problem:

How to return Clara safely to her family… without letting Morvath steal the girl away.

### Rejection and Opportunity

### A Fire Between Allies

The night settled quietly over the forest as the party gathered around the fire, the crackling flames casting long shadows across weary faces. What had once been a group of strangers had become something more—companions bound by shared trials, victories, and loss.

In that rare moment of calm, truths were spoken.

Rellim pressed Elyndor about the nature of his patron. Elyndor admitted he did not truly understand the being that guided him—only that it was radiant, distant, and spoke through dreams. It offered guidance, warnings, and purpose, though its true identity remained unknown. Despite the uncertainty, Elyndor carried a quiet faith in it.

The group lingered there, speaking more openly than ever before—sharing fragments of their pasts, their doubts, and the paths that had brought them together.

But peace never lingers long.

The party turned their attention to Clara.

At the edge of the slums, Ace, Evander, and Elyndor went ahead to retrieve her parents. They found them quickly and led them back, attempting to explain what had happened—how their daughter had been taken, how she had been lost… and how she had been brought back.

When Clara’s parents saw her, the moment shattered.

Her mother wept. Her father stood frozen.

But neither of them saw their daughter.

They saw something changed. Something unnatural.

A demon. A possession. A mockery wearing Clara’s face.

No words could convince them otherwise.

They rejected her completely.

And they walked away.

Ace and Evander quietly guided the parents back toward the slums, while the rest of the party remained behind with Clara—now alive, but abandoned.

In the aftermath, the party made a difficult decision.

Morvath would remain behind in Cinderdeep, training Clara to survive in a world that had already cast her aside. If she could no longer return to her old life, then she would need the strength to carve out a new one.

Before parting, the group shared farewells with the strange and unpredictable fairy.

When Kaelthar embraced Morvath, something subtle shifted—something faint in the air, almost imperceptible, yet lingering enough to leave a question unanswered.

Then Morvath and Clara prepared to walk their own path.

### The Refuge Grows

The party returned to the cavern refuge, where the freed prisoners had begun to settle.

Durgin, the dwarf, greeted them eagerly, asking about supplies. The group distributed food and resources throughout the camp, bringing relief to many who had only recently escaped the horrors of the Pit.

They then sat with Hammond, recounting everything that had transpired—Ebonmere, the Godswood, the hag, the Fey Realm, and the strange turn of fate that had led them here.

Rellim revealed the Scrolls of Irillis.

Hammond’s reaction was… restrained. If the knowledge meant something to him, he chose not to show it.

An invitation.

## Session 17 - 3/18/26

### Masks of Wealth, Chains of Power

### A City of Silk and Silence

Morning came without warning.

Not with danger. Not with steel. But with quiet.

Ebonmere stirred gently, its streets alive yet restrained, as though the city itself wore a mask—composed, controlled, and hiding something beneath. The calm was unnatural. In a place built on shadow and ambition, silence was never a comfort.

It was a warning.

The party prepared for what lay ahead—the Harvest Festival Auction, a gathering of power cloaked in luxury.

Kaelthar and Rellim set out first, seeking proper attire befitting the company they were about to keep. At the Silver Thread, they were measured, fitted, and refined into something presentable—something that could move unnoticed among nobility. Deep blue fabrics, tailored lines, and careful presentation ensured they would not stand out… nor be dismissed.

Because tonight, appearances would matter.

Their path led next to the trade hall—a place of constant motion, where deals were struck and fortunes shifted with a word.

There, they met Vorash Mirefang and Zerak Blackscale once more.

The two dragonborn greeted them warmly, gratitude still fresh for the freedom the party had granted them. In return, they offered gold—five hundred pieces—and something far more valuable:

A favor.

One that could be called upon in the future.

But it was not the gold that lingered.

It was what Rellim saw.

Across the hall, workers bore a mark upon their foreheads—a symbol burned into flesh, subtle at first glance, but impossible to ignore once seen. Too many wore it. Too many for it to be coincidence.

Ownership.

Control.

Slavery, hidden beneath the polished surface of Ebonmere.

And then came the whispers.

A failed caravan. A shipment lost to trolls. Silverhold casting blame.

And beneath it all, something darker.

A final auction item. Unlisted. Unseen.

“Something… special.”

Something that breathes.

Before leaving, the party revealed the sapphires recovered from the hag’s lair—gems belonging to none other than Lord Maelrix Veynar.

The reaction was immediate.

Relief. Excitement. Recognition.

In exchange for their return, the party secured a promise: should they deliver the Seeing Stone, they would be granted an audience with Lord Veynar himself.

A door had opened.

Now they only needed to step through it.

### Fortune and Risk

While Kaelthar and Rellim navigated politics, the others sought opportunity.

Ace, Evander, and Elyndor ventured into the slums, returning to Stix’s shop where potion supplies awaited. Among them, something rare—a potion of flight—was traded for the strange remains of fairy wings.

The party negotiated from there. Their auction debt was forgiven, and the legendary map and broom of flying came into their possession as part of the arrangement. Further reward, including the matter of House Veynar's heirloom blade and the God's Eye, was tied to the investigation still ahead.

Before they left, Rellim suggested a performance.

If Sylvara's spies were watching, House Veynar needed to appear finished with them.

So the guards threw them out.

Publicly. Loudly. With enough contempt to sell the lie.

### The Slums of Ebonmere

At the counter, the barkeep held the message beneath his palm and demanded payment.

La Panada.

The party prepared for a trap.

A note was tied around Kuzco's neck, asking for help if trouble found them. Kaelthar took to the air on the newly acquired broom, scouting from above. He saw no Sylvara at first, but two figures waited on rooftops where ordinary patrons had no reason to be.

### The Contract Remains

On the rooftops, Rellim found one of Sylvara's assassins and placed a blade beneath his jaw. With the man's life held in a thin line of pressure, Rellim forced him down into the alley and brought him before his mistress.

The party pressed Sylvara for the truth.

Who hired her?

Who paid for Seralyth's assassination?

What power stood behind the explosion at House Veynar?

Aethon Ardyn still lived.

The contract still stood.

Kill him, and make it look like a robbery gone wrong.

The party left with no clean answer, only another blade placed in their hands. To uncover the master behind Seralyth's death, they would have to decide how far into Sylvara's game they were willing to walk.

Part III ended not with victory, but with survival bought at a terrible price.
